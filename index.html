Baking Tips

Little secrets from our kitchen to yours.

Room Temp Butter

Always use room-temperature butter for smooth, even creaming — cold butter causes lumpy batters.

Weigh Your Flour

A scale is your best friend. Scooping flour can add up to 30% more — leading to dense, dry bakes.

Rest Your Dough

Cold-resting cookie dough overnight develops deeper flavour and prevents overspreading in the oven.

Oven Thermometer

Most ovens run 15–25°F off. An inexpensive thermometer can completely transform your results.
